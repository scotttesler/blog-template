Film is inconvenient in all the right ways. You cannot check the shot. You cannot take forty versions of the same doorway. You have to decide, then keep walking.

I took an old point-and-shoot through the neighborhood market, a side street with laundry lines, and a park bench that faces nothing special. Half the roll will be soft. A few frames will feel like they belong to someone more patient than me.

That is enough reason to finish the roll.
