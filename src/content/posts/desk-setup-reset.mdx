Every few months the desk accumulates evidence of unfinished intentions: sticky notes, cables, a mug that became storage. The reset is simple and slightly ceremonial.

Everything off. Wipe the surface. Put back only what earns daily use. One notebook. One pen that works. The lamp. The rest goes in a box labeled later, which is honest enough.

A clear desk does not write the draft. It does make the draft feel possible again.
