I am halfway through a novel I keep recommending and a nonfiction book I keep arguing with in the margins. The third book is short essays for nights when concentration is thin. The fourth never made it past chapter two, and that is fine.

Reading lists are not scoreboards. They are weather reports. This month is slower, more reread-heavy, less interested in finishing for the sake of finishing.

If a book earns a place on the nightstand twice, it stays.
