I used to treat rest like a leftover: whatever remained after the list was done. The list is never done.

Better rest is scheduled and slightly protected. A walk with no podcast. A nap without apology. An evening where the phone charges in another room.

None of this is radical. It is maintenance. The work gets sharper when the person doing it is allowed to stop on purpose.
