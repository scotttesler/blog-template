Sunday night is for the odds and ends. Carrot tops, a lonely leek, the end of a Parmesan rind that has been waiting in the freezer like a promise.

Nothing about this is fancy. Salt early, taste late, and let the pot do the work while you wipe down the counters. When it is done, freeze a few portions before you eat the first bowl. Future-you will be grateful for something warm that does not require a grocery run.
