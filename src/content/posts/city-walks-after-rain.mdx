title: City walks after rain

After a storm the city smells like metal and leaves. Traffic thins for a while. Reflections turn ordinary storefronts into something almost cinematic.

I walk without headphones. The point is not steps or a route. It is noticing which blocks feel different when the air is clean and the gutters are still running.

Bring a jacket you do not mind getting damp. Leave the perfect photo for someone else. The walk is the souvenir.
