I used to pack for imaginary versions of myself: formal dinners, sudden hikes, three outfits per day. The bag always won.

Now the rule is simpler. One pair of shoes I will actually walk in. Clothes that all work together. A small pouch for cables and nothing else that needs a story.

The goal is not minimalism as identity. It is arriving somewhere and not spending the first hour sorting what I should have left at home.
