The best cafe tables are not the ones with perfect espresso. They are the ones where the power outlet is already claimed by someone who looks settled, and you still find a corner that works.

I pack light: laptop, one notebook, headphones I rarely use. Noise is part of the deal. So is the quiet social pressure to keep moving when the work stalls.

Stay long enough to finish one real thing. Tip well. Leave before the afternoon rush turns the room into a waiting room with laptops.
